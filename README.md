## 5. Verifying the Discount Applied

A field accepting the code without an error message is **not** proof the discount registered. The only reliable confirmation is a visibly lower order total.

- Enter the code and click apply.
- Look directly at the order total, not just the field itself.
- If the total dropped, the discount is confirmed.
- If it's unchanged, don't proceed to payment — retry or troubleshoot first.
- Save the confirmation email if the discount appeared at checkout but seems missing from the final invoice.

## 11. Total Cost Beyond the Invoice

The invoice price is only part of the real cost of a proxy purchase. Also worth factoring in:

- **Failed request cost** — a pool with a lower success rate against your targets means paying for attempts that return nothing usable.
- **Troubleshooting time** — hours spent debugging an unreliable setup are a real cost, even without a line item on the bill.
- **Missed or delayed results** — data collected too late, or not at all, can carry its own downstream cost.

A lower invoice price paired with a lower success rate can end up more expensive, in practice, than a marginally higher-priced option that performs reliably.
